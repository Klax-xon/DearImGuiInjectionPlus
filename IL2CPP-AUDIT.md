### Architectural Summary
The migration to BepInEx 6 IL2CPP (`net6.0`) has been forensically audited, hardened, and verified against both compile-time and in-game runtime hazards:
- Native DirectX 11 (`IDXGISwapChain::Present` / `Present1`) and DirectX 12 (`ID3D12CommandQueue::ExecuteCommandLists`) hooks in `RendererFinder` operate purely against native COM VTables via `Reloaded.Hooks` and `SharpDX`, remaining completely insulated from IL2CPP runtime overhead.
- Custom `MonoBehaviour` types ([`UnityMainThreadDispatcher`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/BepInEx/UnityMainThreadDispatcher.cs), [`DearImGuiInjectionPlusRunner`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/BepInEx/DearImGuiInjectionPlusRunner.cs), [`TestPluginRunner`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/TestPlugin/TestPluginRunner.cs)) implement the required `public MyType(IntPtr handle) : base(handle)` native pointer constructors and register idempotently via `ClassInjector.RegisterTypeInIl2Cpp<T>()` prior to instantiation.
- CoreCLR memory ordering in [`StateSnapshotPipeline<T>`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/StateSnapshotPipeline.cs#L15-L61), [`StructSnapshotPipeline<T>`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/StateSnapshotPipeline.cs#L68-L137), and [`FrameGate`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/RendererFinder/FrameGate.cs) uses `Volatile.Read`/`Volatile.Write` and `Interlocked` primitives appropriately for `x64` RyuJIT.
- Multi-layer input blocking intercepts Win32 window messages, C++-inlined `UnityEngine.Input` methods (`GetKeyInt`, `get_mousePosition_Injected`, `get_mouseScrollDelta_Injected`), `Unity.InputSystem` devices, and the low-level `[RequiredByNativeCode]` engine bridge `UnityEngineInternal.Input.NativeInputSystem.NotifyUpdate` (`NativeInputEventBuffer`), preventing 3D gameplay mouse look/click bleed-through and stuck keys across live passthrough toggles.

## 2. Subsystem Breakdown & Forensic Verification

### 2.1 Entry Point & `MonoBehaviour` Lifecycle
- **`BasePlugin` Transition ([`DearImGuiInjectionPlusBaseUnityPlugin.cs`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/BepInEx/DearImGuiInjectionPlusBaseUnityPlugin.cs), [`TestPlugin.cs`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/TestPlugin/TestPlugin.cs)):**
  - Both plugins inherit from `BepInEx.Unity.IL2CPP.BasePlugin`, override `Load()` and `Unload()`, and extract Unity frame-tick callbacks (`Update`, `LateUpdate`, `OnApplicationQuit`, `OnDestroy`) into dedicated `MonoBehaviour` runners ([`DearImGuiInjectionPlusRunner`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/BepInEx/DearImGuiInjectionPlusRunner.cs), [`TestPluginRunner`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/TestPlugin/TestPluginRunner.cs)).
- **Native Pointer Constructors & `ClassInjector` (`AUD-06` — Resolved):**
  - [`UnityMainThreadDispatcher.Register()`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/BepInEx/UnityMainThreadDispatcher.cs#L38-L53) captures `_mainThreadId = Thread.CurrentThread.ManagedThreadId` and checks `ClassInjector.IsTypeRegisteredInIl2Cpp<UnityMainThreadDispatcher>()` before calling `ClassInjector.RegisterTypeInIl2Cpp<UnityMainThreadDispatcher>()`.
  - [`DearImGuiInjectionPlusBaseUnityPlugin.Load()`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/BepInEx/DearImGuiInjectionPlusBaseUnityPlugin.cs#L86-L108) registers and attaches `UnityMainThreadDispatcher` and `DearImGuiInjectionPlusRunner` **before** applying Harmony patches or initializing native SwapChain hooks.
- **Native-Managed Object Lifetime & `HideFlags` (`AUD-05` — Resolved):**
  - Both `_runnerGo` instances use `HideFlags.HideInHierarchy` with `Object.DontDestroyOnLoad(_runnerGo)` instead of `HideFlags.HideAndDontSave`, ensuring Unity's scene manager reliably invokes `OnApplicationQuit()` and `OnDestroy()` during engine shutdown.
  - `Shutdown(bool fromOnDestroy = false)` and `Teardown(bool fromOnDestroy = false)` skip calling `UnityEngine.Object.Destroy(go)` when `fromOnDestroy == true`, preventing re-entrant native destruction.

### 2.2 HarmonyX Detours, C++ Inlining & 3D Gameplay Input Blocking
- **Value-Type Return Overrides ([`UnityInputBlocker.cs`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/Patches/UnityInputBlocker.cs)):**
  - Under HarmonyX's `Il2CppDetourMethodPatcher` on `x64`, `UnityEngine.Vector2` (8 bytes) and `UnityEngine.Vector3` (12 bytes) are blittable structs with identical memory layouts across CoreCLR and IL2CPP C++. HarmonyX's managed detour trampoline safely writes `ref __result` / `ref __0` into the native return buffer/register without stack corruption.
- **IL2CPP Method Stripping Isolation & `NativeMethodInfoPtr` Validation (`AUD-01`, `AUD-02` — Resolved):**
  - [`ApplyHarmonyPatchesSafely()`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/BepInEx/DearImGuiInjectionPlusBaseUnityPlugin.cs#L158-L274) resolves class-level `declaringType` fallback (`attr.info.declaringType ?? classDeclaringType`), queries methods and properties with `BindingFlags.Public | BindingFlags.NonPublic | BindingFlags.Static | BindingFlags.Instance`, and checks `Il2CppInteropUtils.GetIl2CppMethodInfoPointerFieldForGeneratedMethod(targetMethod)` to verify that the backing `NativeMethodInfoPtr_*` field exists and is non-zero before detouring.
  - Patches targeting the same `MethodBase` (such as `BlockMousePosition` `[HarmonyPrefix]` and `TrackMousePosition` `[HarmonyPostfix]` on `Input.get_mousePosition` and `Input.get_mousePosition_Injected`) are merged into a single `_harmony.Patch(targetMethod, prefix, postfix)` call.
- **Root Cause & Resolution of 3D Gameplay Mouse Look / Click Bleed-Through (`AUD-08` — Resolved):**
  1. **Win32 `GetRawInputBuffer()` Bypasses `WndProc` (`WM_INPUT`):** On Windows, `UnityPlayer.dll` drains high-frequency mouse deltas (`lLastX`, `lLastY`) and mouse button transitions (`RI_MOUSE_LEFT_BUTTON_DOWN`, `RI_MOUSE_RIGHT_BUTTON_DOWN`) directly from the kernel queue via `GetRawInputBuffer()` in its C++ pump before `DispatchMessageW` invokes `InputManager.SubclassHandler`. Consequently, 3D gameplay mouse blocking relies on intercepting the input inside Unity after `GetRawInputBuffer()` runs.
  2. **IL2CPP C++ Inlining of `Input.GetKey` and `Input.mousePosition`:** In `UnityEngine.InputLegacyModule`, `Input.GetKey(KeyCode)`, `Input.GetKeyDown(KeyCode)`, and `Input.GetKeyUp(KeyCode)` are 1-line wrappers around private methods `GetKeyInt(KeyCode)`, `GetKeyDownInt(KeyCode)`, and `GetKeyUpInt(KeyCode)`. Similarly, `Input.mousePosition` and `Input.mouseScrollDelta` wrap `get_mousePosition_Injected(out Vector3)` and `get_mouseScrollDelta_Injected(out Vector2)`. During IL2CPP C++ compilation (`GameAssembly.dll`), `/O2` optimization inlines the outer wrappers at call sites (including `KeyCode.Mouse0`–`Mouse6` and `KeyCode.W`). [`UnityInputBlocker`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/Patches/UnityInputBlocker.cs#L70-L248) now patches both the public properties/methods and the private `GetKeyInt`, `GetKeyDownInt`, `GetKeyUpInt`, `get_mousePosition_Injected`, `get_mouseScrollDelta_Injected`, and `get_mousePositionDelta_Injected` targets.
  3. **`UnityEngineInternal.Input.NativeInputSystem.NotifyUpdate` Hook (`UnityEngine.InputModule.dll`) & `NewInputSystemBlocker`:** In IL2CPP games using `Unity.InputSystem`, `UnityLinker` frequently strips `InputSystem.DisableDevice` if the game never calls it directly, and `NewInputSystemBlocker` previously ignored selective capture (`WantCaptureMouse` / `WantCaptureKeyboard` when `BlockAllInputsWhenOpen == false`) and lacked `BindingFlags.DeclaredOnly` when resolving `Mouse.current` vs `Pointer.current`. [`NewInputSystemBlocker.ApplyNativeInputHook()`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/Patches/NewInputSystemBlocker.cs#L40-L168) now hooks `UnityEngineInternal.Input.NativeInputSystem.NotifyUpdate(NativeInputUpdateType, IntPtr eventBuffer)`—which is marked `[RequiredByNativeCode]` and never stripped in IL2CPP—and zeroes `NativeInputEventBuffer.eventCount` (offset `8`) and `sizeInBytes` (offset `12`) when blocking all inputs (or filters `InputEvent.type` by `deviceId` during selective capture). Because `NotifyUpdate` still executes with `eventCount = 0`, `Unity.InputSystem` automatically resets per-frame `Mouse.current.delta` to `(0, 0)` while dropping all raw mouse deltas and clicks.

### 2.3 Root Cause & Resolution of Stuck Keyboard Keys Across Passthrough (`AUD-09` — Resolved)
- **Three Compounding Causes Identified:**
  1. **Unrecorded Key/Button Presses During Passthrough:** [`InputManager.CaptureDepressedKeys()`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/InputManager.cs#L399-L413) was only invoked when the overlay initially opened (`OnCursorVisibilityToggled(true)`). When operating in passthrough mode (`BlockAllInputsWhenOpen == false` and `!WantCaptureKeyboard`), [`InputManager.SubclassHandler`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/InputManager.cs#L260-L335) forwarded `WM_KEYDOWN` / `WM_LBUTTONDOWN` to `Comctl32.DefSubclassProc` without calling `SetKeyDown(vk)`. When the user later released the key after disabling passthrough, `CheckAndClearKey(vk)` returned `false` and `SubclassHandler` swallowed the physical `WM_KEYUP` message, leaving the key permanently stuck in Unity!
  2. **Premature `ClearAllDownKeys()` Before Asynchronous `PostMessage(WM_KEYUP)` Delivery:** When synthesizing key releases via `User32.PostMessage(hWnd, WM_KEYUP, ...)` while the overlay remained open (`IsCursorVisible == true`), immediately calling `ClearAllDownKeys()` in `ReleaseRemainingDepressedKeys` caused `SubclassHandler` to see `_downKeys == 0` when the posted `WM_KEYUP` arrived on the Win32 message queue, swallowing the synthesized `WM_KEYUP` before Unity could process it.
  3. **Missing Engine-Level Input State Reset on Passthrough Transition:** Disabling passthrough while holding `W` did not trigger `Input.ResetInputAxes()` or `InputSystem.ResetDevice()`.
- **Resolution Implemented:**
  - [`InputManager.SubclassHandler`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/InputManager.cs#L287-L331) now records `SetKeyDown` for every `WM_KEYDOWN`, `WM_SYSKEYDOWN`, and mouse `*BUTTONDOWN`/`*BUTTONDBLCLK` message allowed through during passthrough.
  - [`InputManager.FlushDepressedKeysOnCaptureTransition()`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/InputManager.cs#L415-L442) is invoked immediately when [`DearImGuiInjectionPlus.BlockAllInputsWhenOpen`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/DearImGuiInjectionPlus.cs#L71-L82) transitions from `false` to `true`, when `WantCaptureMouse`/`WantCaptureKeyboard` transitions from `false` to `true` in [`DearImGuiInjectionPlus.NewFrame()`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/DearImGuiInjectionPlus.cs#L621-L635), and in [`DearImGuiInjectionPlusRunner.Update()`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/BepInEx/DearImGuiInjectionPlusRunner.cs#L15-L26).
  - [`InputManager.ReleaseRemainingDepressedKeys(IntPtr hWnd, bool keepTrackedForSubclassPassThrough)`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/InputManager.cs#L512-L579) preserves `_downKeys` bits when `keepTrackedForSubclassPassThrough == true` (while `IsCursorVisible == true`) and sets `_pendingKeyReleaseFlushFrames = 2`, allowing the posted `WM_KEYUP` / `WM_*BUTTONUP` messages to pass `CheckAndClearKey` in `SubclassHandler` and `NotifyUpdatePrefix` in `NewInputSystemBlocker`.
  - [`UnityInputBlocker.TryResetInputAxes()`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/Patches/UnityInputBlocker.cs#L43-L56) invokes `Input.ResetInputAxes()` and [`NewInputSystemBlocker`](file:///d:/BepInEx%20stuff/DearImGuiInjectionPlus-BE6/DearImGuiInjectionPlus/Patches/NewInputSystemBlocker.cs#L256-L269) invokes `InputSystem.ResetDevice()` on the Unity main thread to zero held axes and keys inside `UnityPlayer.dll` immediately.
